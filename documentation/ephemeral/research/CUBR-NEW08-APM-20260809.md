# Preregistration: competed identity-init APM on the value-rail geomix/ctxmix coders (NEW-08, narrowed)

**State:** PREREGISTERED DESIGN — no implementation or measurement result is
recorded here. Committed to `main` before the candidate is built, per
protocol. Probe basis: `probes-20260809/probe-new08-notes.md` (PR #55).

## Scope (narrowed from the original NEW-08)

The original "SSE over any backend" scope is empty — cm2, geocm, ppmd,
record_cm and cm already carry competed SSE chains (verified in source, PR
#55). The live hole is the **value-stream rail's symbol-level coders** —
`BwtGeoMix` (scheme 11) and `BwtContextMix` (scheme 10) — which produce
probabilities with no calibration stage. Their beneficiaries are the MED16
nested streams that win `mr` and `x-ray` at the operating point.

## Mechanism

One identity-initialised APM stage refining the per-symbol (per-bit of the
range-coded stream) probability of the geomix — and, if the same plumbing
covers it at negligible marginal cost, ctxmix — coder:

- 33-knot interpolated table over quantised `stretch(p)`, context = low bits
  of the coder's existing symbol context, ≤512 rows total;
- identity initialisation: untrained the stage is a no-op, so it cannot hurt
  cold blocks — the probe measured this is precisely why it *gains* in the
  production 64 KB-reset regime (+10.6% analogue) more than continuous
  (+4.4%);
- **competed per block**: the encoder codes each block both ways and keeps
  the smaller, recording one flag bit in the block's scheme header. By
  competitive-min construction the output can never be larger than the
  incumbent by more than the flag overhead, and the flag lives inside the
  compressed block payload the decoder already parses versioned.

## Wire semantics (stated up front, F13 lesson)

Archives that never select the APM variant remain byte-identical to today's.
Archives where a block selects the APM need the new decoder; the old decoder
must **fail closed** on the unknown flag, not corrupt. This is
one-directional compatibility, same class as the `web` preset's tbits field,
and ships under the same rule: choosing it is a decision about who can open
the result — here made per-block by the encoder, so the property is:
new-encoder output is readable by old decoders **only** for files where the
APM never wins. This is acceptable for a research lever behind the
competitive rail; whether it enters a shipping preset is a product decision
taken after measurement, not here.

## Ceiling (stated before build)

From the probe, discounted for its weaker analogue base (~10% weaker than
the real med16→BWT+geomix chain): realistic band **−1% to −3%** on the two
target files' archive bytes; the analogue's 64 KB-reset figure (+10.6%) is
the optimistic bound and is NOT expected to be realised. On all other corpus
files the expected effect is exactly 0 (competitive flag never set) — any
nonzero regression is a defect, not a trade.

## Falsifiable prediction

On the full Silesia files, campaign binary lineage, preset `max`, byte-exact
round-trip on every observation:

1. `mr`: ratio 0.20776 → **≤ 0.20568** (−1.0% relative or better)
2. `x-ray`: ratio 0.42919 → **≤ 0.42490** (−1.0% relative or better)
3. Every other meta-36 corpus file: archive byte-identical to the pre-change
   build, or smaller.
4. **NO-GO rule:** if both mr and x-ray improve by less than 0.5%, the lever
   is a NO-GO regardless of any other property.

## Speed side (mandate rule: density-speed trades are product decisions — both sides stated)

The APM adds one interpolated lookup + one update per coded bit on the geocm
decode path, which is **98.2% of x-ray decode time** (decode-attribution run,
PR #54). Predicted decode-wall impact on x-ray at `max`: **≤ +10%**; measured
and reported per file either way. If the measured slowdown exceeds 10% while
the density gain holds, the result is reported as a trade for the operator to
place, not silently accepted or rejected.

## Gates (all must pass before any number is read)

  commit (any new failures are the implementation's, fix before measuring).
- New focused tests: APM identity at init (stage is a no-op untrained);

- Bench runs on the quiet dev-ai stand, campaign thread semantics, pinned to
  cores 16–19 (campaign pin 0–15 untouched), per-file figures only.
